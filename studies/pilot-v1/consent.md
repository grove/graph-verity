# Local pilot consent — read before creating any form

You are invited to try a local experimental RDF search and exact-plan interface.
The purpose is to find usability, relevance and understanding problems. This is
an exploratory convenience-sample pilot, not a production evaluation. Participation
is voluntary. You may decline any rating, skip a task, stop, or request deletion
without giving a reason or losing anything. Adults only; no compensation is promised.

The operator will observe task choices and record only an anonymous random study
code, chosen task language, broad RDF experience and language proficiency, task
completion/abandonment, assistance, elapsed seconds, structured correctness and
understanding checks, explicit approval/cancellation, and your optional 1–5
confidence and Find relevance ratings. Confidence means confidence in your own
interpretation, not the search score. You explain the preview **before** approving.
The operator assesses the explanation without recording its wording.

Use only supplied public/synthetic task data. Do not enter personal, employer,
confidential or credential information. The tool does not collect queries, names,
email addresses, demographics, screen/audio recordings, quotes, browser history,
IP addresses, session URLs/tokens, keystrokes, private RDF or raw explanations.
It sends nothing to an external service. No recording or quotation is authorized
by this consent. Those would require a separately designed protocol and explicit
separate consent; this kit cannot store them even if requested.

Forms stay on the operator's machine in `study-private/`, with restricted file
permissions, for at most **30 days after the session**, then are deleted including
operator backups. You can use your study code to request earlier deletion from
the operator who invited you. Keep that code if you want to withdraw later;
there is no contact list or way to identify you without it. The operator explains
this directly and gives you their existing contact method outside the tool.

Only aggregate counts may later be considered for a public report. Small groups
can still be recognizable; the operator must review that risk with participants
before publishing, avoid identifying combinations, and suppress identifying
breakdowns. No individual form or raw participant data is published. There is
no automatic submission or publication. Withdrawing deletes your local form;
already published anonymous aggregates may not be retractable individually.

The operator asks whether you understand this and freely agree to the structured
local observations and aggregate use. Only an affirmative answer permits
`init --consent`. That flag records the operator's attestation, not a signature,
proof of understanding or an ethics approval. Give the participant the code
and this notice. Do not start if they decline or have unanswered concerns.
